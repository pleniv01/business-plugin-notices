# Pee.ai ChatGPT skill privacy

Effective November 11, 2026. This skills-only plugin uses fictional,
nonidentifying examples to prepare questions about test instructions and labels.
It does not request or analyze personal test results, health records or account data.
It has no MCP server, execution scripts, telemetry, publisher storage or automatic
transmission to Pee.ai. The publisher receives and retains no conversation data
through this plugin. OpenAI processes conversations under the user's account
settings and policies; this is not a promise of no OpenAI retention.

Optional visits to https://pee.ai are separate and follow that site's policies.
No personal data is added to links. The plugin does not invoke paid features,
retrieve comparison data, make diagnoses or recommend treatment. It does not
target children under 13. For product information or contact options visit
https://pee.ai; do not send medical records or credentials in support requests.
